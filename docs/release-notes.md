**Install:** download the `.dmg` below, open it and drag **Veer** into **Applications**. Veer runs on macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Veer isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time you open it:

- **macOS 15 or later:** click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, find "Veer was blocked to protect your Mac", click **Open Anyway** and confirm with Touch ID or your password.
- **macOS 14:** Control-click Veer in Applications, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

For audio and video, install FFmpeg: `brew install ffmpeg`. Images, PDFs and text work without it.

Veer lives in the menu bar. Start dragging files in Finder and hold **Shift** to convert, **Command** for tools or **Control** for recipes. The README has the full guide.
